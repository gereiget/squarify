Likha & 400 Score

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This privacy policy covers the Likha & 400 Score Android app (the "App"). The App is a score keeper for the card games 400 and Likha. It runs entirely on your device.

Summary

  • The App does not collect, store, send or share any personal information.
  • The App does not connect to the internet or to any server.
  • The App's only permission is notifications. They are created on your device, work offline, and are used only to show the score and let you update it without opening the App.
  • The App has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third party SDKs.
  • The App has no accounts or sign in.

Information we collect

None. Player names and scores you type into the App are kept only in the App's local storage on your device so you can continue a game. This data never leaves your device and we cannot access it.

Permissions

The only Android permission the App requests is permission to post notifications. The App does not request internet access, location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage.

Notifications

The App uses a notification so you can quickly see the current score and update it straight from the notification without opening the App. These notifications are created locally on your device and work completely offline. The App does not use push notifications or any notification server, and no data is sent anywhere. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings, and the App will keep working normally.

Third parties

The App has no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or other third party services, so no data is shared with anyone.

Deleting your data

You can delete all data saved by the App at any time by clearing the App's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the App.

Children's privacy

The App collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact the developer through the contact details listed on the App's Google Play Store page.
